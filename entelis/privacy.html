ENTELIS Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Everything you put into ENTELIS stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. We cannot see your data, because it never reaches us.

Who we are

ENTELIS is made by Saud bin Abdullah, the same independent developer behind the Al-Himmah app. In this policy, "we" means the developer of ENTELIS.

What ENTELIS uses, and where it stays

  • Your entries. What you log in Talk (sleep, wake-up time, mood, water, money, plans and notes) is saved on your iPhone, encrypted with a key kept in the iPhone Keychain.
  • Your voice. When you tap Speak, speech is turned into text on your iPhone only. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Understanding what you said. Your words are read by Apple's on-device model to fill in your card. This happens on your iPhone, not in the cloud.
  • Apple Health. With your permission, ENTELIS reads sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, exercise minutes and active energy. It never writes to Health. Health data is used only to show you your own patterns, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with anyone.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share any personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • ENTELIS has no account and makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Membership is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether your membership is active. We never receive your name, email or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings, or from the Membership section inside the app.

Your export file

You can export your data to a file protected by a passphrase you choose. The file is yours: we never see it, and where you keep it is up to you. Without the passphrase, nobody can open it, including us, so keep it somewhere safe.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything from the Your data section in the app at any time. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing left for us to delete.

Children

ENTELIS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, at any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change ever affects how your data is handled, the app will tell you before it takes effect.
